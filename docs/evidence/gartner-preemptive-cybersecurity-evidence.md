## 1. Gartner official statements (with quotes)

### 1.1 Core definition and headline prediction — Press release, 18 Sep 2025

**Title:** "Gartner Says that in the Age of GenAI, Preemptive Capabilities, not Detection and Response, are the Future of Cybersecurity"
**URL:** https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-09-18-gartner-says-that-in-the-age-of-genai-preemptive-capabilities-not-detection-and-response-are-the-future-of-cybersecurity
**Date:** 18 Sep 2025 (Gartner newsroom); wire distribution 19 Sep 2025 (BusinessWire ID 20250919589679; mirrored on Yahoo Finance, Morningstar, Nasdaq, MarketScreener, GuruFocus, CXOToday, Globe and Mail).
**Type:** Gartner official, free.

Verbatim key statements:

- "By 2030, preemptive cybersecurity solutions will account for 50% of IT security spending, up from less than 5% in 2024, replacing standalone detection and response (DR) solutions as the preferred approach to defend against cyberthreats, according to Gartner, Inc." **[multi-source verbatim]**
- "Preemptive cybersecurity technologies use advanced AI and machine learning (ML) to anticipate and neutralize threats before they materialize. It includes capabilities such as predictive threat intelligence, advanced deception and automated moving target defense." **[multi-source verbatim]** — this is the canonical Gartner definition and the three named capability examples.
- Carl Manion, Managing Vice President, Gartner: "Preemptive cybersecurity will soon be the new gold standard for every entity operating on, in, or through the various interconnected layers of the global attack surface grid (GASG)." **[multi-source verbatim]**
- Manion: "DR-based cybersecurity will no longer be enough to keep assets safe from AI-enabled attackers. Organizations will need to deploy additional countermeasures that act preemptively and independently of humans to neutralize potential attackers before they strike." **[multi-source verbatim]**
- "Gartner predicts that by 2030 there will be over 1 million documented cybersecurity Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs), up 300% from approximately 277,000 in 2025." **[multi-source verbatim]**
- "There will be a shift from broad, one-size-fits-all DR security platforms toward more targeted and effective preemptive cybersecurity solutions, many of which will be based on agentic AI and domain-specific language models (DSLMs)." **[multi-source verbatim]**
- Manion: "Because no single vendor can effectively address the entirety of the GASG, partnerships and interoperability between specialized solutions will become even more crucial." **[single-snippet verbatim]**
- Manion (as quoted by Cybersecurity Dive from the same release): "For example, a vendor that specializes in using preemptive security for Internet of Things in the healthcare industry might need to integrate their technology with a cloud-based electronic health records platform." **[single-snippet verbatim, via third party]**
- Closing pointer: "Non-clients can read more in the Gartner article, Don't Delay in Building Preemptive Cybersecurity Solutions, and view the complimentary webinar on-demand, Preemptive Cybersecurity – A Top 5 Disruptive Trend in Cybersecurity for 2025." **[single-snippet verbatim]**

Not found in this release: any mention of CTEM, "exposure management", "predict-prevent-detect-respond", or the "3 D's". Those appear in other Gartner artifacts (below).

## 2. CTEM — Gartner's official public description

### 2.1 Free Gartner article
**Title:** "How to Manage Cybersecurity Threats, Not Episodes"
**URL:** https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/how-to-manage-cybersecurity-threats-not-episodes
**Topic page:** "Cybersecurity Threats: Key Insights, Trends, and Best Practices" — https://www.gartner.com/en/cybersecurity/topics/cybersecurity-threats
**Type:** Gartner official, free.

Five stages as captured from the gartner.com index **[single-snippet verbatim fragments]**:
1. **Scoping** — "define the scope of the CTEM initiative by understanding what is important to business counterparts and what impacts warrant remedial effort".
2. **Discovery** — "a discovery process to identify assets within that scope and their risk profiles".
3. **Prioritization** — "helps cut out the noise, with prioritization based on urgency, severity, ability to remediate and level of risk posed to the organization, allowing the team to clearly articulate which business-critical systems it will prioritize".
4. **Validation** — "uses controlled simulation or emulation of an attack to understand how malicious actors could execute on cybersecurity threats, relying on manual assessment activities such as red team or penetration testing exercises, or leveraging automated assessments such as breach and attack simulations, and also assesses suggested remediation approaches for their efficacy and organizational feasibility".
5. **Mobilization** — "aims to reduce friction in the approvals for treatments, inviting organizations to define communication standards and document cross-team approval workflows".

Topic-page framing: "Security and risk teams are embracing continuous threat exposure management (CTEM), an integrated, iterative approach to prioritizing and continually refining security posture improvements." and "Gartner predicts that through 2026, nonpatchable attack surfaces will grow to include more than half of the enterprise." **[single-snippet]**

### 2.2 The 2026 breach prediction — two official wordings
- Press release "Gartner Identifies the Top Cybersecurity Trends for 2024" (22 Feb 2024) — https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-22-gartner-identifies-top-cybersecurity-trends-for-2024 : "By 2026, Gartner predicts that organizations prioritizing their security investments based on a CTEM program will realize a two-thirds reduction in breaches." **[multi-source verbatim]**. Same release defines CTEM as "a pragmatic and systemic approach organizations can use to continually evaluate the accessibility, exposure and exploitability of digital and physical assets, aligning assessment and remediation scopes with threat vectors or business projects rather than an infrastructure component." **[single-snippet verbatim]**
- Research note "Implement a Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM) Program" (21 Jul 2022; Jeremy D'Hoinne et al.) — https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/4016760 (paywalled). Vendors (Vectra, SimSpace, SafeBreach, Team Cymru, One Identity, Hadrian) quote it as: "Organizations prioritizing their security investments based on a continuous threat exposure management program will be three times less likely to suffer a breach by 2026." **[third-party quotation of a paywalled note; "3x less likely" and "two-thirds reduction" are the same claim]**. One third-party (Vectra) explicitly notes the prediction is "directionally supported, but not empirically validated."
